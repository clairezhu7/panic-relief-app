A desktop accessibility app that provides immediate support for users experiencing panic attacks, whether triggered by anxiety-related conditions or stressful events

**Key Features**
- Deep Breathing: A follow along deep breathing exercise with calming, intuitive visual animations. Users may choose between two breathing patterns (Box Breathing and 4-7-8 Breathing), both of which are proven to slow down heart rate and reduce stress hormones.

- 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding: An interactive grounding exercise that prompts users to observe and describe their surroundings through sight, touch, sound, smell, and taste. By focusing on the five senses, the exercise encourages users to pull away from overwhelming thoughts and ground themselves in the present.

**Accessibility Features**
- Features a translucent widget that is always visible in the corner of the screen, ensuring instant access to the app without interfering with normal device use
- Keyboard shortcut for opening the app: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd>
- Users can set a preferred exercise (Breathing or Grounding) as their default landing page to minimize menu navigations during panic attacks (Settings &#8594; General)
- Users can customize app defaults, such as music preferences, further reducing unnecessary clicks during emergencies (Settings &#8594; Breathing / Grounding)

**Installation Guide**
To install Mindstep Relax, go to the latest release (V1.0.0) and click on the .exe file to download the installer!
